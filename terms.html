Terms of Use

Effective 28 August 2026 · Bariton (iOS)

Bariton is a voice training tool, not a medical device. It promises no result, and no exercise should ever be done through pain.

1. What Bariton is

Bariton measures the fundamental frequency of your voice and offers vocal training exercises, available through a subscription or a one-time purchase.

2. What Bariton is not

Bariton is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disorder of the voice, speech or hearing, and it does not replace the advice of a health professional. The measurements shown are indicative.

3. No guaranteed result

Results depend on individual practice. Bariton promises no change to your voice, and no timeframe for obtaining one.

4. Safety

You agree to stop any exercise immediately in the event of pain, discomfort or hoarseness, and to consult an ENT doctor or a speech therapist if any difficulty persists. Exercises are never done by forcing.

5. Subscription

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled at least 24 hours beforehand. Payment is charged to your Apple Account on confirmation of purchase. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings. A free trial, where offered, is attached to one named plan.

6. What stays free

Bariton keeps a free tier, permanently and without a time limit:

  • Unlimited measurements. A measurement is a dedicated take that produces a numerical result — your median frequency, your range and your stability. There is no daily limit and there never will be.
  • Your full history and progress curve, with no limit on the number of entries.
  • One complete exercise with its audio demonstration.

This is a commitment, not a promotional offer: we will not move these features behind the paywall later. If the free exercise ever has to be replaced for technical reasons, it will be replaced by another complete exercise, never removed.

Two clarifications, so that the commitment is unambiguous rather than merely broad. The live frequency shown while you follow a guided exercise is part of the subscription and is not covered by the free tier — it is not a measurement in the sense above. And the commitment covers the features listed here, as they exist on the date of publication: new analysis features that may be added later (timbre, resonance, fine-grained stability, comparison between takes) may form part of the subscription. Nothing promised here is taken back.

Bariton mesure la fréquence fondamentale de votre voix et propose des exercices d'entraînement vocal, accessibles par abonnement ou par achat unique.

Bariton n'est pas un dispositif médical. Il ne diagnostique, ne traite et ne prévient aucun trouble de la voix, de la parole ou de l'audition, et ne remplace pas l'avis d'un professionnel de santé. Les mesures affichées sont indicatives.

Vous vous engagez à interrompre immédiatement tout exercice en cas de douleur, de gêne ou d'enrouement, et à consulter un ORL ou un orthophoniste en cas de trouble persistant. Les exercices ne se font jamais en forçant.
