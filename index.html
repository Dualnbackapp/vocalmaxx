Questions

What does voice pitch in Hz measure?

Hertz describes frequency. A pitch estimate concerns the recorded voice sample, not attractiveness, confidence or a medical diagnosis. Recording conditions affect comparisons.

Can I practise without forcing my voice lower?

Yes. Focus on comfortable breath, articulation, resonance and pace. Stop if practice causes pain, irritation or hoarseness and seek professional advice for persistent concerns.
