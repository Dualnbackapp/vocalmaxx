What you can do

VocalMaxx: voice pitch analysis and guided vocal practice

01

See your pitch

Record a short voice sample to explore pitch in Hz, stability and range. Recording conditions can change the result.

02

Hear the exercise

Explore guided drills with audio examples across breath, resonance, articulation and pace.

03

Compare yourself with yourself

Follow recordings over time under similar conditions. Focus on comfortable practice, without chasing a universal voice score.

VocalMaxx is not a medical device. Stop practice if it causes pain, irritation or hoarseness, and seek professional advice.

Questions

What does it cost?+

The App Store and the app show the current price and available purchases in your local currency before confirmation. Trial availability depends on the offer and your eligibility. A free download does not mean every feature is free.

Is the app available in my language?+

These are translated website pages. Check the Languages section of the App Store listing for the app itself. Some content may use a different language.

How do I manage a subscription?+

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Select the app to review or cancel an active subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
