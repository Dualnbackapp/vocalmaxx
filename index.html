Bariton

Measure your voice. Train it. Hear the difference.

Your voice never leaves your iPhone. Audio is analysed on the device and discarded straight after. No account, nothing uploaded.

What it does

Bariton measures the median frequency of your speaking voice, then gives you guided exercises — each one with an audio demonstration you listen to before you try it. You are measured against yourself, session after session. No score, no ranking, no comparison to anyone else.

What it is not

Bariton is a voice training tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any voice disorder, and it promises no result. Stop any exercise in case of pain or hoarseness, and see an ENT doctor or speech therapist if discomfort persists.
